Create a parent-managed financial literacy app for Nigerian children aged 10–18.

The Problem

Create a parent-managed financial literacy app for Nigerian children aged 10–18. The app helps parents manage allowances, monitor spending behaviour, and teach children practical money skills in a safe, supervised environment. It is inspired by GoHenry in the UK but fully localized for Nigeria’s adaptation. The product focuses on control, visibility, and learning, rather than complex banking features.

The key challenge was:

Teaching financial skills without using real money.

Client

The Design Challenge

    Teaching financial skills without using real money. Allowing kids to practice, not just learn facts.

The Approach

Key design decisions included:

  • Different UI design for parent and child versions.
  • Adding sound effects for the child version.

The Solution

An Android app that is between a banking app and learning app.
